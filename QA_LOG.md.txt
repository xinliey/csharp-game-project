# Game Testing Log – 2025/12/30

## Session Goal
Long play session to identify gameplay, system, and progression issues.

## Issues Identified

1. Part-Time Job Reward Logic  
   - Extra score is not being awarded to Jay during part-time job gameplay.

2. Quest and Lore Interaction Conflict  
   - Quest interactions and lore-only interactions are currently overlapping.
   - During lore interactions, no quest progression or score updates should be triggered to avoid incorrect level and state changes.

3. UI Readability – Player Phone  
   - Font size in the player’s phone UI is too large and should be reduced for better readability.

4. Progression Pacing – Jay  
   - Jay’s interaction triggers activate too late in the progression flow.
   - Adjust trigger threshold to activate at Level 3 to improve pacing and player engagement.

5. NPC Availability – Ethan  
   - Ethan appears too infrequently on the map, making it difficult for players to interact with him.
   - Adjust schedule so Ethan remains available for a longer period after school.

6. Score Balance Adjustment  
   - Add bonus score for Ethan and Jay when the player arrives at school early.
   - These characters require faster progression compared to other NPCs.

7. Quest Reset Logic  
   - When resetting the game, ensure all quest-related `isDone` booleans are properly reset to prevent broken quest states.

8. Cutscene Trigger Review  
   - Recheck and document how each cutscene trigger functions, including conditions and dependencies.
